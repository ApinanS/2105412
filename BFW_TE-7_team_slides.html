Stage 5 · Do it

The root cause, as a mechanism

WH-1 FV-104 stuck at ≈ 50 % — stem or positioner does not follow FIC-104 OUT

FV-104 stem / positioner stuck at ≈ 50 % → at steady 100 m³/h nobody notices → the boiler house raises the load → TT-101 dips → TIC-101 and FIC-104 drive OUT to 100 % → the valve stays at 50 %; only the larger header-to-shell ΔP (the shell pressure falls 105 → 88 kPa g) lets a little more steam through (10 780 kg/h against 11 915 needed) → TT-101 settles at 65.3 °C.

Proved by: Position 50 % against OUT 100 %; T-1 stem 50 % locally; T-2 OUT 100 → 30 % moved nothing; surface, header and meters all clean

Stage 6 · Look back

Lessons and the ledger

Do again

Compare the valve position with the controller output first.
Use the clean values at the new flow (NTU, ΔP ∝ F²) to clear the exchanger.
Stroke the valve as a small, reversible test.

Never again

Never retune a controller that is already at 100 % OUT.
Never assume a valve moves because its output moved.

USD
Total spentUSD 730
Value recoveredUSD 5,000

Rule for the next shift

If a controller is at 100 % and the valve position has not moved, stroke the valve before you touch the tuning — a stuck valve hides at steady load and shows only when the load changes.
