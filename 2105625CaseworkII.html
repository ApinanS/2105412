C · HAZOP of the reactor node

Where does one line of a HAZOP come from?

The logic

A HAZOP line is a chain of eight answers

1

Node

Which part of the plant?

2

Design intent

What should it do, in numbers?

3

Parameter

Flow, temperature, pressure, level…

4

Guide word

No, less, more, reverse…

5

Deviation

Parameter + guide word

6

Causes

Why could it happen?

7

Consequences

Where does it end if nothing stops it?

8

Safeguards and actions

What protects us? What to add?

Read left to right, top to bottom. Each answer feeds the next.

Vocabulary

Guide words and what they mean

Guide wordMeaningReactor example
NoComplete negation of the intentNo coolant flow
LessQuantitative decreaseLess feed flow
MoreQuantitative increaseMore temperature
As well asSomething extra is presentImpurity as well as feed
Part ofOnly part of the intent is achievedPart of the feed reacts
ReverseOpposite of the intentReverse flow in the feed line
Other thanSomething else happensOther material than feed

Design intent

Hold the batch near 62 °C (limit 70 °C) and below 3 bar(g) for the batch time.

A deviation only exists relative to an intent, so put numbers in the intent.

Step 2 · generic example

Guide word + parameter = deviation

More  +  temperature  =  More temperature

In numbers: above 65 °C is a deviation, and 70 °C is the design limit.

Which parameter on your P&ID matches your guide word?

Step 3 · the origin of the causes

Causes come from the energy balance

$$\rho V c_p\frac{dT}{dt}=\underbrace{(-\Delta H)\,k_0e^{-E_a/RT}\,C\,V}_{\text{heat generated}}-\underbrace{UA\,(T-T_c)}_{\text{heat removed}}$$

Generation up

Higher $C$ (extra charge), higher $k$ (more catalyst, hot feed)

Removal down

Lower $UA$ (fouling, poor mixing), higher $T_c$, low coolant flow

Step 4 · generic example

Follow the deviation until something stops it

Temperature above 70 °C

→

Rate ×2.3 per +10 K

→

Generation outruns removal

→

Vapour and gas raise pressure

→

PSV lifts or vessel fails

$\dfrac{k(T+10)}{k(T)}=\exp\!\left[\dfrac{E_a}{R}\left(\dfrac{1}{T}-\dfrac{1}{T+10}\right)\right]\approx 2.3$  for $E_a=80$ kJ/mol, $T\approx 335$ K
Write the worst credible end of the chain, as if no safeguard acts.

Step 5 · generic example

Safeguards and recommendations

Prevent

Cooling margin, temperature control, charge limits.

Detect

High-temperature alarm (TAH) and operator response.

Mitigate

High-high trip (TSHH), relief valve (PSV).

List only safeguards that exist on your P&ID. Recommend an action only if the risk is still too high.
